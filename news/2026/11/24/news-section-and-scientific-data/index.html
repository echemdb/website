    echemdb has a news section, and our data descriptor was accepted in Scientific Data

    Welcome to the new news section of echemdb.org! From now on, we announce new data, software releases, publications, and events here.

    We open it with good news: the data descriptor of the echemdb CV dataset was accepted in Scientific Data.

    The data descriptor presents the dataset of fingerprint cyclic voltammograms (CVs) for single crystal electrodes, digitized from the literature, annotated with metadata, and standardized into unit-aware Data Packages. It also describes the framework behind it: the svgdigitizer to recover data from published figures, the metadata schema, and the unitpackage Python API to explore and reuse the data.

    The publication is the result of a joint effort of many contributors, who digitized, annotated, and validated the data. Thank you all!
