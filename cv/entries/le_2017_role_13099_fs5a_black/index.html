    Cu(100) - \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ NaOH }\)

    echemdb identifier: le_2017_role_13099_fs5a_black
    tags: BCV

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Cu(100) recorded in \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ NaOH }\) at a scan rate of \(10 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure s5a in C. S. Le Duff et al. Role of the adsorbed oxygen species in the selective electrochemical reduction of CO2 to alcohols and carbonyls on copper electrodes. Angewandte Chemie, 129\(42\):13099–13104, 2017

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Cu(100) electrode was prepared by:

    • Electropolished in 66% orthophosphoric acid at +2 V.
    • Rinsed with ultra-pure water.
