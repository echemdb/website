    Pt(10 9 8) - \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ HClO4 }\)

    echemdb identifier: abe_2019_electro-oxidation_315_f2b1_black
    tags: BCV

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Pt(10 9 8) recorded in \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ HClO4 }\) at a scan rate of \(50 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 2b1 in K. Abe et al. Electro-oxidation of CO saturated in 0.1 M HClO4 on basal and stepped Pt single-crystal electrodes at room temperature accompanied by surface reconstruction. Surfaces, 2\(2\):315–325, 2019

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Pt(10 9 8) electrode was prepared by:

    • Annealed in hydrogen/oxygen flame
    • subsequently cooled in air down to 473 K
    • protected with water
    • immediately transferred into the electrochemical cell.
