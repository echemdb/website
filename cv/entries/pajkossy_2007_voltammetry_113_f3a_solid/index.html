    Ir(100) - \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ KClO4 }\)

    echemdb identifier: pajkossy_2007_voltammetry_113_f3a_solid
    tags: BCV

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Ir(100) recorded in \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ KClO4 }\) at a scan rate of \(10 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 3a in T. Pajkossy et al. Voltammetry and impedance measurements of Ir\(100\) electrodes in aqueous solutions. Journal of Electroanalytical chemistry, 600\(1\):113–118, 2007

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Ir(100) electrode was prepared by:

    • High temperature annealing at about 1900°C in a nitrogen-hydrogen atmosphere for 1 min.
    • Cool down in the gas stream for 2 min.
    • Transferred to the electrochemical cell under protecting gas stream.
    • Hanging meniscus configuration.
