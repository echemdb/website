    Pt(110) - \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ KOH }\)

    echemdb identifier: markovic_1996_oxygen_6715_f1_110wide
    tags: BCV

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Pt(110) recorded in \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ KOH }\) at a scan rate of \(50 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 1 in N. M. Markovic et al. Oxygen reduction on platinum low-index single-crystal surfaces in alkaline solution: rotating ring disk Pt\(hkl\) studies. The Journal of Physical Chemistry, 100\(16\):6715–6721, 1996

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Pt(110) electrode was prepared by:

    • flame annealed.
    • cooled in hydrogen atmosphere. Preparation reference: https://doi.org/10.1021/j100011a001
