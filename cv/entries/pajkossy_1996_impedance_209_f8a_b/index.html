    Au(100) - \(0.05 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ KClO4 }\) + \(0.05 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ NaBr }\)

    echemdb identifier: pajkossy_1996_impedance_209_f8a_b
    tags: BCV , SHA

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Au(100) recorded in \(0.05 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ KClO4 }\) + \(0.05 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ NaBr }\) at a scan rate of \(10 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 8a in T. Pajkossy et al. Impedance aspects of anion adsorption on gold single crystal electrodes. Journal of Electroanalytical Chemistry, 414\(2\):209–220, 1996

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Au(100) electrode was prepared by:

    • Annealed with a Bunsen burner
    • Cooled in air.
    • Quenched with Milli-Q water.

    Comment left by the curator on the published figure Main peak does not fit the y-axis limits.
