    Ir(111) - \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ HCl }\)

    echemdb identifier: pajkossy_2005_voltammetry_69_f8a_solid
    tags: BCV , SHA

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Ir(111) recorded in \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ HCl }\) at a scan rate of \(50 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 8a in T. Pajkossy et al. Voltammetry and impedance measurements of Ir\(111\) electrodes in aqueous solutions. Journal of Electroanalytical Chemistry, 582\(1\-2\):69–75, 2005

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Ir(111) electrode was prepared by:

    • High temperature annealing at about 1900°C in a nitrogen hydrogen atmosphere for 100s.
    • Transferred to the electrochemical cell under 5N argon or in a 6N nitrogen stream.
    • Hanging meniscus configuration.

    Comment left by the curator on the published figure the curve is a bit noisy.
