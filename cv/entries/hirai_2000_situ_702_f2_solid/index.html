    Fe(110) - \(0.05 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ Na2SO4 }\)

    echemdb identifier: hirai_2000_situ_702_f2_solid
    tags: BCV

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Fe(110) recorded in \(0.05 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ Na2SO4 }\) at a scan rate of \(50 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 2 in N. Hirai et al. In situ electrochemical atomic force microscopy with atomic resolution of Fe\(110\) in sodium sulfate aqueous solution. ISIJ international, 40\(7\):702–705, 2000

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Fe(110) electrode was prepared by:

    • Created from a single crystalline rod.
    • Orientation by Laue backscattering, mechanically polished and etched.
    • details see https://doi.org/10.2355/isijinternational.40.702
