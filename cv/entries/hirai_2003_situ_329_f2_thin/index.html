    Pb(100) - \(0.05 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ H2SO4 }\)

    echemdb identifier: hirai_2003_situ_329_f2_thin
    tags: BCV

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Pb(100) recorded in \(0.05 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ H2SO4 }\) at a scan rate of \(1 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 2 in N. Hirai et al. In situ EC-AFM observation with atomic resolution of Pb\(100\) and Pb\(111\) single crystals in sulfuric acid solution. Journal of power sources, 113\(2\):329–334, 2003

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Pb(100) electrode was prepared by:

    • Chemically polished with a mixed solution of acetic acid and hydrogen peroxide.
    • Washed with ethanol.
