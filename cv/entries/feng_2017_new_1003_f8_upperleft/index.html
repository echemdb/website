    Pt(POSC) - \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ H2SO4 }\)

    echemdb identifier: feng_2017_new_1003_f8_upperleft
    tags: BCV

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Pt(POSC) recorded in \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ H2SO4 }\) at a scan rate of \(10 \; \mathrm{V\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 8 in Z. Feng et al. New advances in ohmic microscopy. Russian Journal of Electrochemistry, 53:1003–1010, 2017

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Pt(POSC) electrode was prepared by:

    • POSC prepared by melting the tip of the wire using a hydrogen flame.
    • Annealed in a methane flame continuously for 24 to 48 h.
