    Pt(poly) - \(0.5 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ H2SO4 }\)

    echemdb identifier: arulmozhi_2018_design_113_f8_spherical
    tags: BCV

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Pt(poly) recorded in \(0.5 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ H2SO4 }\) at a scan rate of \(50 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 8 in N. Arulmozhi et al. Design and development of instrumentations for the preparation of platinum single crystals for electrochemistry and electrocatalysis research part 3: final treatment, electrochemical measurements, and recommended laboratory practices. Electrocatalysis, 9:113–123, 2018

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Pt(poly) electrode was prepared by:

    • Flame and oxygen annealed before measurement.
    • details see https://doi.org/10.1007/s12678-016-0331-0.
