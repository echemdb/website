    Au(111) - \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ H2SO4 }\)

    echemdb identifier: cuesta_2000_adsorption_5684_f3a_black
    tags: BCV

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Au(111) recorded in \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ H2SO4 }\) at a scan rate of \(10 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 3a in A. Cuesta et al. The adsorption of sulfate and phosphate on Au\(111\) and Au\(100\) electrodes: an in situ STM study. Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics, 2\(24\):5684–5690, 2000

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Au(111) electrode was prepared by:

    • Annealed in a hydrogen flame for 6 min.
    • Cooled down in nitrogen stream.
