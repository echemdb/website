    Pt(111) - \(0.05 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ H2SO4 }\)

    echemdb identifier: strmcnik_2010_enhanced_880_f1tl_black
    tags: BCV

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Pt(111) recorded in \(0.05 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ H2SO4 }\) at a scan rate of \(50 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 1tl in D. Strmcnik et al. Enhanced electrocatalysis of the oxygen reduction reaction based on patterning of platinum surfaces with cyanide. Nature chemistry, 2\(10\):880–885, 2010

    Further information

    The figure shows raw data.

    The Pt(111) electrode was prepared by:

    • Inductively heated in a mixed 3% hydrogen / argon atmosphere at 1100 K for 10 min.
    • Cooled slowly in that atmosphere.
    • Covered with a droplet of MilliQ water.
