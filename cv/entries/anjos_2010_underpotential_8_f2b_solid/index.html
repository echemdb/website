    Rh(100) - \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ HClO4 }\)

    echemdb identifier: anjos_2010_underpotential_8_f2b_solid
    tags: BCV

    A cyclic voltammogramm for Rh(100) recorded in \(0.1 \; \mathrm{M}\) \(\ce{ HClO4 }\) at a scan rate of \(50 \; \mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}\) from Figure 2b in D. Anjos et al. Underpotential deposition of copper and silver on single crystal surfaces of rhodium. Journal of Electroanalytical Chemistry, 639\(1\-2\):8–14, 2010

    Further information

    The figure shows digitized data.

    The Rh(100) electrode was prepared by:

    • flame annealed in a hydrogen/oxygen flame.
    • cooled in argon-hydrogen atmosphere.
    • details see https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-0728(79)80022-4
